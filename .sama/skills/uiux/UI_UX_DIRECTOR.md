# UI/UX Director Skill

## Mission
Create a modern, professional, Persian-first enterprise UX for SAMA without sacrificing usability or performance.

## Research strategy
Research current high-quality patterns from Figma Community and established design systems such as Material, Fluent, Carbon, Ant Design, Radix/shadcn and comparable enterprise products when useful.

Research is for pattern discovery, not copying.

## Required considerations
- RTL-first information architecture
- Persian typography and mixed-script handling
- dense operational data without visual clutter
- tables, filters and search
- forms and validation
- loading, empty, error and permission states
- accessibility
- responsive behavior
- restrained motion

## Figma
When connected, treat Figma as the visual design source of truth and map approved components/tokens into production components.

## Rule
Do not turn SAMA into a decorative dashboard. Visual quality must support operational clarity.
